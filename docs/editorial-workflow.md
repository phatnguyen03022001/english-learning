# Phase 6 — Editorial Decisions and Authority

Canonical governance: `docs/editorial-policy.md` (unchanged). No workflow service,
auth platform, automatic reviewer promotion or time-based acceptance is introduced.

## Separate axes

- Storage: a draft may pass JSON Schema, graph and referential checks.
- Editorial: claim-specific review is accepted, rejected or deferred; `editorialStatus`
  may remain draft/provisional/disputed while evidence is incomplete.
- Publication: all dependencies, current rights and verified scoped authority are required.
  `verified` alone can never waive any gate.

Role categories in V2 include linguistic, grammar, bilingual, rights, lead_editorial and
data_integrity. A commit author/integration writer is not appointed by being able to push.
`reviewerId`, `appointmentReference` and even an "accepted" outcome remain *claims*
until the owner’s appointment, scope, effective period and revocation are independently
established. The publication gate only accepts an explicitly trusted authorization
verifier; no such genuine appointment is encoded by Phase 6.

## A review cycle

1. Prepare candidate draft and preserve canonical identity. Export review queue with the
   exact subject IDs, field paths, missing evidence and source rights to verify.
2. Independently check linguistic claims, Vietnamese alignment, grammar, idiomatic examples
   and source reuse scope. A domain reviewer cannot approve outside assigned scope.
3. Record an `EditorialReview` with claim-specific scope and references to authority;
   `EditorialDecision` with outcome, subjects, action and optionally supersession.
   A `retain` action can be a legitimate `non_transition` decision.
4. Conflicts or evidence gaps remain `disputed` or `deferred_pending_evidence`.
   No one may self-appoint or self-upgrade lexical claims through the CLI.
5. A split/merge/reconciliation requires predecessor retirement, explicit successors,
   a before/after revision audit and a per-incoming-edge adjudication.
   `LIFECYCLE_SOURCE_NOT_RETIRED`, `LIFECYCLE_INCOMING_EDGE_UNRESOLVED` and
   `DECISION_EFFECT_MISMATCH` are machine diagnostics. Future decisions may
   supersede earlier records, never overwrite the old decision. V2 does not assert
   that a self-reported historical hash authenticates historical existence.
6. A separate publication selection explicitly scopes subjects, closes over relevant
   dependencies and checks that all applicable external authorizations/rights
   have been verified. Rights or data-integrity reviewers may block despite LER approval.

Actual review decisions and appointments cannot be generated by software tests.
